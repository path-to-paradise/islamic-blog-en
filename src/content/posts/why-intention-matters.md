One of the most repeated sayings in Islamic tradition is short enough to fit in a sentence: actions are judged by intentions. Simple to say, easy to skip past, and surprisingly demanding to actually live by.

## Same action, different weight

Two people can donate the same amount, at the same event, to the same cause — one hoping to be noticed, the other hoping no one finds out. Nothing about the outward action tells you which is which. The intention is doing all the real work.

## Checking in with yourself

Intention isn't usually a single decision made once. It's something worth checking periodically, especially with habits that have been running long enough to go on autopilot. A short, honest question — *why am I actually doing this?* — is enough to catch drift before it goes too far.

This isn't about paralysis or overthinking every small action. It's about occasionally slowing down long enough to notice what's actually motivating a habit, and adjusting course if the answer isn't the one you'd want.
