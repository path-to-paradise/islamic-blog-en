This is a very powerful Salawat. Whatever intention you recite it with will come to pass.

You can recite this Salawat for all kinds of needs:

- If your husband or wife is angry and you want to reconcile
- If you want someone to be kinder toward you and like you more
- To receive a promotion at work
- For healing and health problems
- To increase your energy and cleanse yourself
- For difficulties or blockages in your life
- And for anything else you may need

## Salawat

> **Allahumma salli ala sayyidina wa mawlana Muhammad**  
> **wa ala ali sayyidina wa mawlana Muhammad**  
> **afdala salawatika bi adadi malumatika**  
> **wa barik wa sallim**

## Translation

> *O Allah, send Your finest blessings upon our master and patron Muhammad and upon the family of our master and patron Muhammad, in a number equal to all that is encompassed by Your knowledge. Bless them and grant them peace.*
